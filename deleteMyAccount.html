How to Delete Your SMB Account

If you wish to permanently delete your SMB account, follow these simple steps below. Please note that deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

  • 1

    Open the SMB app and log in to your account.

  • 2

    Tap the hamburger menu (☰) at the top-left corner of the app.

  • 3

    Scroll down and select the "Delete Account" option.

  • 4

    Confirm your decision when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted.

Tip: Make sure to back up any important data before deleting your account, as all data will be lost after deletion.
